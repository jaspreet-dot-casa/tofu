## Pull requests

- **Never mark a review comment or thread as resolved.** Reply to it and push the
  fix, then leave it open. Resolving is the reviewer's call, not mine — marking it
  resolved presumes their point is settled and hides it from their queue before
  they have agreed it is done.
- This applies to every reviewer, human or bot (CodeRabbit, Claude Code Review,
  lint bots). A bot finding is still someone else's comment.
- Un-resolving a thread I resolved earlier is fine; resolving one is not.
